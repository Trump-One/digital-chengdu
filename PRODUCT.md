# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Implementation delegated by the user's explicit instruction to start. Independent Sites project, React/Vinext, Three.js, D1 and R2.
## Users
People who want to explore and collaboratively build a digital Chengdu.
## Product Purpose
Walk through a three-dimensional reconstruction of Tianfu Third Street, inspect buildings and contribute corrections, photographs or replacement models. Expand geographically after the pilot.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Start with a 300–500 m demonstration street segment. Prefer free, replaceable data and tools. Preserve provenance and distinguish surveyed facts from estimated heights and illustrative appearance. Store community contributions durably and separately from the base map. Website first; desktop and HarmonyOS are not this release.
## Evidence on Hand
OSM footprints are downloaded and attributed. On 2026-10-13 the user requested more realistic Funian Plaza and a public URL. Published exterior and location references guide a labeled procedural reconstruction; no photographs are bundled and no live imagery or surveyed model exists. Public viewing is requested; contribution writes remain authenticated.
## Product Principles
Real geography before invented detail. Keep original data intact. Contributions remain attributable and reversible. Free sources must retain their licenses. Show data dates.
